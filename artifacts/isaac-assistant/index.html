ISAAC Record Companion

Built to run on your own Claude subscription — ISAAC has no model and no AI subscription of its own. Nothing is wired up in this build; read the state below before the controls.

Not connected to a model or an MCP server. This companion's call surface is unimplemented in this build. It is not disabled, throttled, or waiting — no request is made and no result is cached.
  • The ISAAC connector must be added to the organization by a Team Owner.
  • Each person then connects and authorises it in their own Claude settings. Nobody can do that step for you.
  • Finalising a record — submit and export — is your act inside ISAAC. No tool here can do it, by construction.

Typed or pasted, deliberately. No supported mechanism for passing an Experiment ID into an artifact by link is documented, so this companion does not pretend to receive one.

Once a call surface exists, this would read the record, its runs, and its open blocking questions, and would write nothing until you confirm a specific value. In this build it reads nothing and reports why.
