## Scope
The question site's page (sign-in, list, one question, done). Mode: Operate. One expert scientist answering on a laptop.

## Direction contract
THESIS: One card, one question, its answer. Refuses the dashboard of chips, panels and stacked context blocks the first version shipped.
OWN-WORLD: Category standard played straight (chosen by the owner): white card on a soft grey ground, near-black text, one blue for the primary action, green only for a saved answer. System sans, 18px body, generous line height; tabular numerals for values.
STORY: Angel reads the question, sees the suggested answer and the one line from his own files behind it, clicks "Yes, that's right" or picks another answer, and moves on. Provenance (beamline, edge, dates, file and line) is always one glance away and never in the way.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Opens straight on the first open question, flashcard style (owner, 23 Nov: "like quiz flashcards ... select the answer, then it shows you the next question"). Thin top line and source line, then "n of N" with a blue progress bar. Centered card: the question as a 30px heading, what it applies to, the reason with its one evidence line, then the answers as full-width numbered choice buttons (suggested first, marked). One click saves and the next card slides in. Below: a "Something else?" field, quiet I don't know / Skip / Add a note, and one "Show the evidence and context" disclosure.
